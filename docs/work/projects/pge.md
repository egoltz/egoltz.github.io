# AI System Audit
This was a graduate capstone project for my MS degree. I led a team that performed an audit on the internal AI system for a F500 regional energy company. 

## Data Storytelling
![User Profiles](/assets/images/pge-user-profiles.png)

My teammate had a general idea of a graphic that he wanted, but couldn’t make. So in a virtual meeting, I had him describe the image he wanted. I asked him a lot of specific questions and sketched a basic mockup on my ipad. We used the mockup to discuss and refine the concepts and then I took my refined mockup and made the final graphic (above) for our slideshow. For the portraits, I worked back and forth between Procreate digital painting app and ChatGPT until I was happy with the images. The finished portraits use a combination of AI and digital painting. For the Confidence, Satisfaction and Frustration score graphs, I used data from our interviews and made the charts in python.
